Questions

The things people ask before they book

How much is this going to cost me?

Every move is priced on what it actually involves: how far, how much, and how hard it is to get in and out. We give you a free written estimate before we touch a single box, and the number we give you is the number you pay.

Do you move out of state?

Yes. Local moves around Stafford and Northern Virginia, and long-distance moves up and down the East Coast. We have taken customers as far as Connecticut.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. You can call and handle the entire move in Spanish, from the first phone call to the last box off the truck.

Do you really clean afterward?

Yes, and it is one of the things customers thank us for most. Ask for it when you book and we will clean the old place once the truck is loaded, which is usually what stands between you and your deposit.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks is comfortable, and the end of the month fills up first. If you are moving sooner than that, call us anyway. We often make it work.

Do you have insurance?

Yes. Ask us on the call and we will send the certificate before your move date, which is also what most apartment buildings and office landlords will require from you.

Tell us where you are going.

Free written estimate, no obligation, and a real person on the phone. Same family, same trucks, since 1980.
